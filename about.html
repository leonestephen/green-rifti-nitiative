About Green Rift

A long-term institution for people, nature and prosperity.

Green Rift Initiative is a community-driven development vision rooted in Ukwala, Siaya County, Kenya.

Our story

From a local idea to a regional ambition.

Green Rift was conceived around a simple belief: rural communities hold enormous potential when people, land, knowledge and capital are connected responsibly.

Our approach is to begin with practical pilots, listen to communities, build credible evidence and develop partnerships that can support long-term scale.

Our mission

To strengthen climate resilience, livelihoods and inclusive economic opportunity through community-led development, environmental stewardship, education and responsible investment.


Our vision

A green, inclusive and prosperous Ukwala that inspires sustainable development across Africa.

Our principles

How we intend to build.

Community first

Local people should understand, shape and benefit from projects affecting their communities.

Evidence led

Baseline data, monitoring and honest learning should guide decisions.

Integrity

Transparent governance, responsible finance and clear accountability are non-negotiable.

Long-term thinking

We design for decades, while delivering practical results in the present.
