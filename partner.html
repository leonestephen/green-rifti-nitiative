Invest & Partner

Help us prove what is possible.

We are seeking founding partners to establish the institution, test practical solutions and build the evidence required for larger-scale investment.

Founding programme

Indicative first-stage funding target: US$100,000–250,000.

This initial capital would support legal establishment, governance, community engagement, baseline studies, pilot projects, monitoring and investment-readiness work.

Green Rift is currently at the founding/pilot stage. Funding figures and impact targets are planning assumptions and will be refined through due diligence and baseline studies.

Ways to partner

Founding funder — support the institutional and pilot foundation.

Technical partner — contribute expertise, research or implementation capacity.

Corporate partner — support youth, environment, digital access or community programmes.

Impact investor — explore future investable commercial projects.

Community partner — help shape and deliver local priorities.

Capital philosophy

Different capital for different purposes.

Grants

Public-benefit programmes, education, research and early pilots.

Partnerships

Corporate and institutional support tied to measurable outcomes.

Impact capital

Scalable projects with social, environmental and financial value.

Commercial capital

Separate ventures for viable businesses in agriculture, energy, technology and value addition.
