Our Work

Practical projects. Measurable outcomes. Scalable ideas.

Our programme connects environmental restoration with livelihoods, skills, innovation and investment readiness.

🌱

Climate-Smart Agriculture

Demonstration farms, soil health, agroforestry, water efficiency, aggregation and value addition.

🌳

Restoration

Indigenous tree nurseries, watershed restoration, biodiversity protection and land stewardship.

👥

Youth Economic Empowerment

Technical skills, apprenticeships, entrepreneurship and digital opportunities.

💧

Water Resilience

Water mapping, conservation, catchment protection and adaptation planning.

☀️

Clean Energy

Explore solar and other clean energy models that improve access and productivity.

🧭

Investment Readiness

Turn validated opportunities into feasibility studies and investable project pipelines.

Implementation

Our first 18 months.

Months 1–3 — Establish

Legal structure, governance, community listening, baseline design and partner engagement.

Months 4–9 — Pilot

Launch civic education, youth skills, demonstration agriculture and the first restoration activities.

Months 10–15 — Measure

Track outcomes, refine delivery models, complete feasibility studies and document lessons.

Months 16–18 — Prepare to scale

Publish results, strengthen partnerships and build the next-stage capital pipeline.
